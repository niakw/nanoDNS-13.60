# Code of Conduct — nanoDNS 13.60

**Language:** English (default) | [Français](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.fr.md)

This applies to the project repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other project-managed spaces. It covers maintainers and contributors equally.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, patient and considerate. Critique technical ideas and evidence, not people. Welcome participants regardless of background, language, experience or accessibility needs. Give constructive, actionable feedback and acknowledge mistakes when they are found.

## Unacceptable behavior

Personal attacks, harassment, intimidation, threats, hate speech, sexual harassment, discriminatory remarks, doxing, repeated unwanted contact and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not share private conversation content, personal identifiers or unredacted console logs without permission.

## Reporting and handling

- For an ordinary misunderstanding or documentation concern, use the relevant [issue template](https://github.com/niakw/nanoDNS-13.60/issues/new/choose).
- For abusive GitHub content, use **GitHub's Report content** controls on the relevant item rather than posting sensitive material publicly. If a private maintainer contact becomes available, it can also be used for discreet reports.
- For **software security vulnerabilities**, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than disclosing exploit details in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or hide inappropriate messages, ask for changes, restrict participation, or close discussions based on severity and repeated behavior, subject to repository and platform permissions. Reports should be reviewed impartially. We do not promise an automatic response deadline.
